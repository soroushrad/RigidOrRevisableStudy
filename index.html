HCI Study Prototype

Rigid or Revisable?

In this study, you will solve four scheduling problems using two different workflow styles. The goal is to understand how people use structured problem-solving workflows when task conditions change.

What will you do?

  1. Read the scheduling constraints carefully.
  2. Follow the workflow steps shown on the left side of the interface.
  3. Place activities into the available time slots.
  4. During the task, an unexpected change may affect your current plan.
  5. Adapt your schedule and continue until the final solution satisfies all constraints.

How are the two workflow styles different?

Rigid Workflow

The strategy steps follow a fixed order. You can adjust the schedule when needed, but the workflow itself cannot be reorganized.

Revisable Workflow

The same structured workflow is used, but after an unexpected task change you can reorganize the remaining strategy steps if you think another order would work better.

Important: You will complete four trials: two with the Rigid workflow and two with the Revisable workflow. Each trial uses a different, similarly structured scheduling problem. The condition order is assigned automatically.

The application automatically assigns a balanced four-trial condition order.
